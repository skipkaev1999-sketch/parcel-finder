    About this map

    A screening tool for finding land parcels near grid infrastructure, built as a portfolio demo. All data is public. Owner names and addresses are left out on purpose.

    How the numbers are computed

    Parcels, power lines, substations and flood zones were loaded into PostGIS and projected to NAD83 / Wisconsin Transverse Mercator (EPSG:3070), which works in metres. Acres come from the polygon area. Distance to the nearest transmission line and substation uses a spatial index nearest-neighbour search. Flood share is the area of the parcel inside the 1% annual chance flood zone divided by the parcel area.

    Accuracy check

    Computed acres match the state's own GIS acres with a median difference of 0.000% (99% of parcels within 0.02%). Distances in the projection differ from ellipsoid distances by 0.04% on average. The same distances computed directly in latitude/longitude degrees would be off by 2.7% on average and by up to 36.8% for east–west distances, because a degree of longitude here is about 27% shorter than a degree of latitude.

    Sources

    • Parcels: Wisconsin Statewide Parcel Map V12 (State Cartographer's Office and Wisconsin Land Information Program), collected January–May 2026, tax roll 2025.
    • Transmission lines, substations, solar plants: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
    • Flood zones: FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer, as published by the Wisconsin Department of Health Services.
    • Basemaps: USGS The National Map.
